# PhoneFold — Privacy Policy

_Last updated 31 August 2026._

**PhoneFold collects nothing.**

There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising identifier, no crash
reporting service and no third-party SDK of any kind. Nobody, including the developer,
can see what you fold.

## What happens on your device

Every fold runs on the device you are holding. The protein you choose, the structure
that comes out of it, the music it makes, the films and the audio you export — all of it
is computed locally and stays there unless you deliberately share it.

## What leaves your device, and only when you ask

**A structure you request by accession.** If you type a UniProt accession, PhoneFold
asks the AlphaFold Protein Structure Database (`alphafold.ebi.ac.uk`) for that
structure. That request contains the accession you typed and nothing else — no
identifier, no device information beyond what any HTTPS request carries. PhoneFold makes
no other network request of any kind.

**Your own iCloud, between your own devices.** PhoneFold keeps a short list of the folds
you have run — which protein, which engine, which style — so that a fold started on your
phone is there on your Mac. It travels through your private iCloud key-value store,
which is your Apple Account and reaches nobody else, and it is a description of what to
fold rather than the fold itself. If you are not signed in to iCloud the app says so and
the list stays on the one device.

**A file you export.** Films, audio, MIDI and mmCIF go where you send them: your photo
library, a folder you choose, or an app you share to. PhoneFold asks for add-only access
to your photo library so that it can save a film, and it never reads your library.

**A fold you share.** If you start a SharePlay session during a FaceTime call, the people
on that call receive the name of the protein you are folding and the settings you are
using. That is the entire payload — no coordinates and no files.

## What PhoneFold is not

PhoneFold is a concert, not a diagnostic tool. It is not a medical device, it does not
give medical advice, and nothing it shows should be used to make a clinical decision.
The folding it draws is a simulation or a generative model, not a measurement of how a
real protein folds; the app says so on screen.

## Children

PhoneFold is rated 4+. It contains no user-generated content, no messaging, no
advertising and no unrestricted web access.
